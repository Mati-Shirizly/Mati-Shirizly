Project Scope

This comprehensive workflow guides the development of a medical device system consisting of:

  • Ultrasound Probe: Hardware device with signal processing, firmware, and connectivity
  • Mobile Application: Real-time imaging interface for iOS/Android with patient management
  • Dashboard Application: Web-based platform for analytics, device management, and reporting

The development follows regulatory requirements (FDA 510(k), CE marking), security standards (HIPAA, GDPR), and quality management systems (ISO 13485, IEC 62304).

Probability: Medium
